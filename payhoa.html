PayHOA

PayHOA simplifies HOA management with modern tools for billing, communication, and compliance — built to reduce manual work, improve transparency, and empower self-managed communities.

My role
Product Designer, then Art Director
Timeline
Jan 2025 – May 2025 (5 months)
Team
Product Owner, Front-end Engineer, Software Engineer

01

Challenge

Homeowners association management has long relied on manual, time-consuming processes and scattered communication tools. These inefficiencies make it difficult to track dues, manage invoices, and maintain accurate financial records — leading to delays, errors, and frustrated communities.

Visit payhoa.com

02

Results

With over 502,000 units managed and 92% positive customer reviews, PayHOA has become the #1 choice for homeowner associations — proving that a well-designed, user-focused platform can transform a traditionally manual industry.

03

Design process

At PayHOA, I led product design from the ground up — aligning with stakeholders, mapping pain points, and designing the initial experience from concept to launch. I created wireframes, built a scalable design system, and ran usability testing to ensure clarity and ease of use for non-technical users.

After launching the first version, I transitioned into the role of Art Director, guiding the product’s direction and mentoring incoming designer Dima as he took over ongoing UX work and iteration.

Deliverables

  • •User research
  • •Competitive analysis
  • •Mid-fidelity prototypes
  • •User testing & product validation
  • •Web app UX/UI
  • •High-fidelity designs

04

Final solutions

Billing, communication and compliance, in one place — the shipped product end to end.

05

Takeaways

  • • Balancing innovation with familiarity is key when redesigning for loyal users.
  • • Small UX wins can yield major user satisfaction gains.
  • • Early investment in design systems pays off in scalability and consistency.
  • • Continuous feedback is essential to sustain design quality post-launch.
